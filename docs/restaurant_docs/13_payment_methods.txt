LA BELLA TAVOLA - PAYMENT METHODS & PRICING

PAYMENT METHODS ACCEPTED

Credit Cards:
- Visa
- Mastercard
- American Express
- Discover
- All major credit cards accepted without additional fees

Debit Cards:
- All debit cards accepted
- No additional fees charged
- Verification may be requested
- PIN entry or signature depending on transaction

Digital Payment Options:
- Apple Pay (via card)
- Google Pay (via card)
- Mobile wallet services
- QR code payment systems (contactless)

Cash Payments:
- Cash accepted for direct payment
- Change provided for cash transactions
- No surcharge for cash payments
- Recommended for large bills due to change availability

PRICING INFORMATION

Average Price Ranges:
- Appetizers: $8-$18
- Pasta dishes: $16-$28
- Seafood entrees: $24-$42
- Meat entrees: $22-$38
- Desserts: $7-$12
- Dessert wines: See wine list for pricing

Lunch Special Menu:
- Reduced pricing during lunch hours (12:00 PM - 3:30 PM)
- Prix fixe lunch options: $18-$25
- Lighter portion sizes available
- Complimentary water and bread service

Dinner Pricing:
- Standard menu pricing as above
- Seasonal specials may vary
- Tasting menu available (chef's discretion pricing)
- Beverage pricing separate from food

ADDITIONAL CHARGES

Service Gratuity:
- Gratuity (tip) not included in bill
- Suggested gratuity: 18-20% of pre-tax bill
- Automatic gratuity (18%) applies to parties 8+
- Gratuity calculated on pre-tax total or included in final bill by customer choice

Splits and Shared Meals:
- Restaurant gladly accommodates shared meals
- No splitting fee charged
- Plates for sharing provided at no charge
- Additional plates charged at $3 each if desired

Tax:
- Sales tax applied to food and beverages
- Current tax rate: 8.5% (varies by location)
- Tax included in displayed pricing breakdown
- Gratuity calculated pre-tax typically

SPECIAL PRICING PROGRAMS

Happy Hour Pricing:
- Monday-Friday: 4:00 PM - 6:00 PM
- Select appetizers: 50% off
- Cocktails: $5-$8
- Wine by the glass: 20% discount
- Beer: $3-$4

Prix Fixe Tasting Menu:
- $65 per person (4-course menu)

Lunch Specials:
- Pasta dishes: $16-$20 (full portions)
- Sandwich specials: $12-$14
- Prix fixe lunch: $18-$25
- Available 12:00 PM - 3:30 PM only

Wine Pairings:
- Standard pairing: $40 per person
- Premium pairing: $60 per person
- Available with tasting menu or regular meal
- Order when making reservation preferred

CHECKS & BILLING PROCEDURES

Single Check:
- Standard presentation as one bill
- Divided among participants or paid by one person
- No additional fees for single check

Split Checks:
- Split checks available upon request
- Specify how to split at time of request (2-way, 3-way, per-person, etc.)
- Processing time slightly longer than single check
- Limit: Split checks per party (typically 4-6 splits maximum)

Check Presentation:
- Check presented when requested
- No rush to pay unless restaurant closing soon
- Payment options confirmed when check brought
- Card taken to processor or cash counted at table

Payment Processing:
- Credit card processing time: 2-3 minutes
- Cash transactions instant
- Card returned on tray with receipt for signature
- Digital payment completed at table via device

DEPOSITS & ADVANCE PAYMENTS

Deposit Requirements:
- $15 per person for parties 8+
- Full invoice estimate for private events
- Non-refundable if cancelled less than 48 hours
- Applied toward final bill if reservation completed

Advance Payment:
- Available for large groups or special events
- Pays for private event rental or food/beverage package
- Difference due at end of meal or credited to account
- Detailed invoice provided in advance

Payment Plans:
- Not typically available for standard dining
- Special arrangements for high-value private events
- Contact manager for special circumstances

Cancellation & Refunds:
- Deposits forfeited per cancellation policy (see separate document)
- Refunds processed to original payment method
- Timeline: 5-7 business days for processing
- Manager approval required for exception refunds

BILLING DISPUTES & ERRORS

Charge Corrections:
- Billing errors corrected immediately
- Manager notified if discrepancy found
- Adjustment made to final bill
- Receipt provided with corrected total

Disputed Charges:
- Discuss immediately with server or manager
- Documentation provided for dispute resolution
- Credit card company can be contacted if unresolved
- Management committed to fair resolution

RECEIPTS & DOCUMENTATION

Receipt Formats:
- Paper receipt provided for cash payments
- Emailed receipt option for credit cards
- Digital record available via loyalty program
- Tax documentation available upon request

Expense Reports:
- Itemized receipt available for business expense documentation
- Cost breakdown by category provided
- Special notations added as needed
- Email copy available for corporate accounts

GRATUITY GUIDELINES

Staff Tipping Standards:
- Server: 18-20% of pre-tax bill
- Bartender: $1-$2 per drink or 18-20% of bill
- Valet: $2-$5 (optional, appreciated)
- Coat check: $1-$2 per coat

Automatic Gratuity:
- Applied to parties of 8+
- Typical rate: 18%
- Separate from any additional tip left
- Mentioned on menu

Tipping Options:
- Card tip: Added to payment when card processed
- Cash tip: Left on table in envelope or folded
- Verbal tip instruction: Tell server amount for card
- No tip option available for those declining
