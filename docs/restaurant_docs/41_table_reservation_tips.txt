LA BELLA TAVOLA - TABLE RESERVATION TIPS & STRATEGIES

GETTING THE BEST TABLE

Table Locations in Restaurant:

Window Seating:
- Most desirable seating
- Natural light during day
- View of Main Street
- Romantic evening atmosphere
- Limited number available
- Request note: "Window table preferred"

Quiet Secluded Tables:
- Away from bar and kitchen
- Peaceful conversation
- Intimate atmosphere
- Romantic occasions ideal
- Back corner locations
- Request note: "Quiet table preferred"

Chef's Counter Seating:
- Front row to kitchen activity
- Interactive dining experience
- Watch chef and cooks work
- Educational food experience
- Limited capacity (4 person max)
- Request: "Chef's counter if available"

Main Dining Room:
- Central location
- Social, vibrant atmosphere
- People-watching possible
- Livelier energy
- Good for group celebrations

Back Section:
- Semi-private feel
- Less foot traffic
- Quieter than main room
- Group-friendly
- Good for private matters

HIGH-TOP/BAR AREA SEATING:
- Casual atmosphere
- Sky-high seating
- Social vibe
- Quick service orientation
- Cocktail-focused area

TIMING FOR BEST SEATING

Peak Hours (Worst Seating Availability):
- Friday 7:00 PM - 8:30 PM
- Saturday 7:00 PM - 9:00 PM
- Both nights heavily booked
- Limited table selection
- Walk-ins unlikely to get choice seating

Off-Peak Times (Best Availability):
- Monday - Thursday all evening
- Sunday afternoon
- Weekday lunch
- Early dinner (before 6:30 PM)
- Late night (after 8:30 PM)

Optimal Reservation Times:
- 5:30-6:00 PM (early seating, quiet)
- Midweek (more availability)
- Lunch service (quieter)
- Weekday (less crowded)

ARRIVAL STRATEGY

Early Arrival:
- 10-15 minutes early shows commitment
- Better seating sometimes available
- Host can accommodate preferences
- Relaxed transition vs. rushed
- Less stress for servers

On-Time Arrival:
- Standard expectation
- 15-minute grace period typical
- Table held if slightly late
- Call if running significant delay
- Courtesy appreciated

Minimizing Wait:
- Off-peak time reservations
- Early seating request
- Straightforward requests (no special setup)
- Quick table turnover realistic
- Waiting area comfortable

ACCESSIBILITY & ACCOMMODATION SEATING

Wheelchair Accessibility:
- Notify at reservation
- Ground-floor seating (entire restaurant)
- Wide pathways to all tables
- Accessible restroom location explained
- Space for wheelchair at table

Hearing Impairment:
- Quieter table location helps
- Seated facing server for lip-reading
- Visible staff attention
- Writing option if needed
- Clear communication

Mobility Concerns:
- Close to entrance preferred
- Avoid stairs (none in restaurant)
- Padded seating comfort
- Back support availability
- Proximity to restroom helpful

HEIGHT/SIZE CONSIDERATIONS:
- Booth seating (fixed, taller people challenging)
- Table seating (more flexibility)
- Spacious seating area
- Comfortable booth width
- Request specifics when booking

CHILDREN & FAMILY SEATING

High Chair Space:

- Away from main traffic

Family Group Seating:
- Adjacent tables for multiple family members
- Sight lines for supervision
