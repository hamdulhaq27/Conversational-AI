LA BELLA TAVOLA - NON-ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES GUIDE

COFFEE BEVERAGES

Italian Coffee Culture:
- Espresso foundation of Italian coffee
- Quick, strong, concentrated shot
- Consumed rapidly (not leisurely sipping)
- Social ritual, not just caffeine
- Quality espresso paramount

Espresso
- Single or double shot
- Dark roast, full-bodied
- 1-2 ounces per shot
- Crema (foam) top important
- Sipped immediately while hot
- Price: $3-$4

Americano
- Espresso shots + hot water
- Longer than espresso
- Milder strength than espresso
- Similar to drip coffee
- Less crema than espresso
- Price: $4

Cappuccino
- Espresso + steamed milk + foam
- Equal parts espresso, milk, foam
- Milk creates sweetness, body
- Often enjoyed for breakfast
- Less common after meals
- Price: $5

Latte
- Espresso + steamed milk (more milk than cappuccino)
- Creamier than cappuccino
- Less foam
- Smooth, velvety texture
- Often afternoon/evening beverage
- Price: $5

Macchiato
- Espresso "marked" with small foam
- Espresso-forward drink
- Small amount of milk foam
- Similar strength to espresso
- Quick afternoon pick-me-up
- Price: $4

Affogato
- Vanilla gelato + hot espresso poured over
- Temperature contrast key
- Dessert and beverage combined
- Spoon and cup necessary
- Melting gelato creates creamy drink
- Price: $8

CAFFEINE CONSIDERATIONS

Caffeine Content:
- Espresso: 60-75 mg per shot
- Cappuccino: 75-120 mg (double shot)
- Latte: 75-120 mg
- Americano: 90-150 mg
- Tea: 20-50 mg typical

Decaffeinated Options:
- Decaffeinated espresso available
- Caffeine-free beverages abundant
- Herbal teas naturally caffeine-free
- All coffee drinks available decaf
- No upcharge for decaf
