LUNCH FAQs

Do you accept reservations for lunch?
Yes, especially for groups or important meetings.

How long is a typical lunch?
45-90 minutes, depending on pace desired.

Can I get my lunch to go?
Yes, takeout available on all lunch items.

Are the lunch prices available for dinner?
No, lunch special pricing only during lunch hours.

Do I need to order the Prix Fixe menu?
No, full menu available; special pricing applies to Prix Fixe.

Can I bring a business associate?
Yes, reservation helps but walk-ins accommodated.
