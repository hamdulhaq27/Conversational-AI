LA BELLA TAVOLA - INGREDIENT SOURCING & PHILOSOPHY

SOURCING PRINCIPLES

Core Philosophy:
- Quality over quantity
- Relationship-based sourcing
- Sustainable practices prioritized
- Supporting local when possible
- Direct sourcing when advantageous

Sustainability Commitment:
- Minimal food waste
- Environmental impact considered
- Responsible fishing practices
- Ethical treatment of animals
- Community food system support

ITALIAN IMPORTS

Key Italian Products:

Pasta (Dried):
- Imported from trusted Italian producers
- Bronze-die extrusion preferred
- Regional Italian pasta specialties
- San Marzano tomatoes when used fresh

Olive Oil:
- Extra virgin from Piedmont and Tuscany regions
- First-cold-pressed preferred
- Direct relationships with producers
- Used for finishing dishes

Cheeses:
- Parmigiano-Reggiano from Parma region
- Fresh mozzarella for specific dishes
- Ricotta for fillings
- Pecorino Romano for pasta dishes

Other Imports:
- San Marzano tomatoes (canned form)
- Italian herbs (dried oregano, basil)
- Balsamic vinegar from Modena
- Italian risotto rice (Arborio, Carnaroli)
