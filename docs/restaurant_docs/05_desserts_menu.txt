LA BELLA TAVOLA - DESSERTS & SWEET TREATS

Our dessert menu features traditional Italian sweets alongside modern creations. All desserts are made fresh in our patisserie.

TRADITIONAL ITALIAN DESSERTS

Tiramisu
The classic "pick me up" - layers of espresso-dipped ladyfingers with mascarpone cream, cocoa powder, and a touch of liqueur. A must-try Italian dessert. Calories: 420 | Contains: Dairy, Eggs, Gluten, Coffee | Vegetarian: Yes

Panna Cotta
Silky smooth Italian custard made with cream and gelatin, served with berry compote and fresh mint. Light and elegant. Calories: 340 | Contains: Dairy | Gluten-Free: Yes | Vegetarian: Yes

Zabaglione con Frutti di Bosco
Light Italian custard made with egg yolks, sugar, and Marsala wine, topped with fresh berries. Served chilled. Calories: 280 | Contains: Dairy, Eggs, Alcohol | Gluten-Free: Yes

Panna Cotta al Pistachio
Silky pistachio-infused cream custard with crushed pistachios and pistachio sauce. Nutty and luxurious. Calories: 380 | Contains: Dairy, Nuts | Gluten-Free: Yes | Vegetarian: Yes

Panettone
Traditional Christmas cake with dried fruits, candied citrus, and raisins. Available seasonally. Sliced fresh or toasted. Calories: 320 per slice | Vegetarian: Yes

Amaretti
Traditional almond cookies from Saronno, delicately crispy exterior with soft interior. Served with dessert wine or coffee. Calories: 120 per cookie | Contains: Nuts, Eggs | Gluten-Free: Typically (check ingredients)

Biscotti di Prato
Hard almond biscotti, perfect for dunking in vin santo or coffee. Traditionally paired with Italian wines. Calories: 140 per cookie | Contains: Nuts, Eggs, Gluten | Vegetarian: Yes

CHOCOLATE CREATIONS

Flourless Chocolate Cake
Rich, decadent chocolate cake made without flour, topped with whipped cream and fresh raspberries. Dense and intensely chocolatey. Calories: 480 | Contains: Dairy, Eggs, Nuts (in some versions) | Gluten-Free: Yes | Vegetarian: Yes

Chocolate Gelato with Biscotti
Premium Italian chocolate gelato served with warm biscotti. Silky and not overly sweet. Calories: 340 | Contains: Dairy | Gluten: Depends on biscotti

Chocolate Mousse Tart
Light chocolate mousse in a crispy tart shell, topped with ganache and fresh berries. Calories: 420 | Contains: Dairy, Eggs, Gluten | Vegetarian: Yes

FRUIT & LIGHTER CHOICES

Grilled Peaches with Mascarpone
Fresh peaches grilled and served warm with creamy mascarpone, honey drizzle, and mint. When peaches are in season. Calories: 280 | Contains: Dairy | Gluten-Free: Yes | Vegetarian: Yes

Mixed Berry Sorbet
Refreshing dairy-free sorbet made from fresh berries, perfect palate cleanser. Calories: 220 | Gluten-Free: Yes | Vegetarian: Yes | Vegan: Yes

Italian Gelato Selection
A rotating selection of premium gelatos made in-house, typically including flavors such as pistachio, hazelnut, stracciatella, and seasonal fruits. Calories: 200-260 depending on flavor | Most are Gluten-Free and Vegetarian

Pesca Melba
Fresh peach dessert with vanilla gelato and raspberry sauce, inspired by the classic Australian dish. Elegant and light. Calories: 320 | Contains: Dairy | Gluten-Free: Yes

COFFEE & AFTER-DINNER

Single scoop of vanilla gelato with hot espresso poured over, creating a warm-cold contrast. Calories: 180 | Contains: Dairy

Zabaglione con Marsala
Traditionally served warm as a dessert or after-dinner comfort. Silky egg custard with Marsala wine. Calories: 240 | Contains: Dairy, Eggs, Alcohol
