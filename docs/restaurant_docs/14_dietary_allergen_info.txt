LA BELLA TAVOLA - DIETARY RESTRICTIONS & ALLERGENS

COMMON DIETARY ACCOMMODATIONS

Vegetarian Options:
- Extensive pasta dishes available vegetarian
- Vegetable-based main courses offered
- Burrata, eggplant parmesan, mushroom wellington available
- Many appetizers vegetarian-friendly
- Notification: Mention at reservation or upon arrival

Vegan Options:
- Plant-based pasta with marinara or oil-based sauces
- Vegetable preparations without dairy or meat stock
- Salads customized without cheese or dressing
- Limited vegan desserts (sorbet available)
- Advance notice (24 hours) recommended for special preparations

Gluten-Free Dining:
- Gluten-free pasta available (+$3 surcharge)
- Many dishes naturally gluten-free (marked on menu)
- Dedicated preparation area considerations
- Cross-contamination possible in busy kitchen
- Severe celiac disease: Inform manager for special precautions

Pescatarian Diet:
- Fish and shellfish preparations available
- Vegetarian options also available
- Avoid meat-based sauces
- Clarify dietary restrictions with server

Kosher Dining:
- Modified menu items may be available
- Advance notice requested (48+ hours)
- Contact manager for specific requirements
- Limited certified kosher options

ALLERGEN INFORMATION

Major Allergens Present in Restaurant:

Shellfish:
- Camarones, calamari, scallops, mussels, clams
- Seafood dishes prepared on same equipment
- Cross-contamination possible
- Notify server of shellfish allergy immediately

Fish:
- Salmon, branzino, dorado, swordfish preparations
- Fish stock used in some sauces
- Separate prep area not available
- Inform server if fish allergy present

Tree Nuts:
- Almonds in pesto sauces and preparations
- Hazelnuts in various dishes
- Walnuts in salads and dressings
- Pistachio gelato and preparations

Peanuts:
- Limited peanut usage in kitchen
- May be present in shared equipment areas
- Inform server of peanut allergy

Dairy:
- Cheese (Parmesan, mozzarella, ricotta) in many dishes
- Butter and cream in sauces
- Gelato with dairy (though dairy-free options exist)
- Some pasta contains egg and dairy

Eggs:
- Fresh pasta contains eggs
- Tiramisu and desserts contain raw eggs
- Some sauces may contain egg
- Gluten-free or alternative pastas available

Gluten:
- Pasta contains gluten unless specified
- Breadcrumbs in appetizers and coatings
- Some sauces may contain gluten-based thickeners
- Gluten-free options available with notice

Soy:
- Limited soy usage in restaurant
- Some Asian-inspired preparations may contain soy
- Inquire with server about specific dishes

PREPARATION FOR ALLERGIC GUESTS

Notification Process:
1. Inform server immediately upon seating
2. Provide details of allergy severity
3. Ask to speak with manager if severe
4. Review menu modifications with kitchen staff

Communication with Kitchen:
- Chef informed of specific allergies
- Special ticket created for allergic guest's order
- Separate preparation area used when possible
- Double-check of plate before serving

Documentation:
- Allergy noted on order ticket
- Kitchen staff briefed on severity
- Server checks with kitchen before serving
- Verbal confirmation given to guest

SPECIFIC ALLERGY PROTOCOLS

Severe/Anaphylaxis Allergens:
- Discuss directly with manager
- Kitchen staff briefed on severity level
- Alternative dishes prepared in isolated area
- Extra care taken to prevent cross-contamination

Mild/Moderate Allergies:
- Standard protocol followed
- Kitchen notified but less intensive precautions
- Avoid dishes with allergen as primary ingredient
- Cross-contamination risk explained to guest

SAFE DISHES BY DIETARY NEED

Gluten-Free Safe Options:
- Grilled branzino with vegetables (check oil)
- Capesante al tartufo (scallops in butter)
- Saffron risotto (no pasta)

- Pasta with gluten-free substitution available

Vegetarian Safe Options:
- All pasta dishes (request without meat)
- Eggplant parmesan
- Burrata and heirloom tomatoes
- Mushroom preparations
- Most appetizers available vegetarian

Dairy-Free Options:
- Grilled seafood with oil-based sauces
- Marinated olives
- Bruschetta al pomodoro

- Sorbet desserts

QUESTIONS & CONSULTATION

Server Consultation:
- Servers trained on basic allergen information
- Can answer questions about prep methods
- Will inform kitchen of concerns
- Honest about unknown information

Chef Consultation:
- Manager or chef available for detailed discussions
- Can describe exact preparation methods
- Knowledgeable about ingredient sourcing
- Best for severe allergies or complex needs

Phone Consultation:
- Call restaurant in advance: +1-800-555-1234
- Discuss dietary needs before reservation
- Chef can prepare special arrangements
- Advance notice (24-48 hours) helpful

THIRD-Party Verified Allergies:
- Some guests bring medical documentation
- Restaurant takes these very seriously
- Extra precautions implemented
- Manager personally oversees preparation

CROSS-CONTAMINATION RISKS

Kitchen Layout:
- Single kitchen for all food preparation
- Shared cutting boards, utensils, and cooking equipment
- One hood vent system
- Difficult to completely eliminate cross-contamination risk

Risk Mitigation:
- Separate prep area when possible
- Clean utensils and boards between orders
- Hand washing between handling allergens
- Communication-driven approach

Honest Communication:
- Restaurant acknowledges cross-contamination is possible
- Cannot guarantee 100% allergen-free environment
- Severe allergies may require external catering
- Anaphylaxis risks discussed seriously with management
