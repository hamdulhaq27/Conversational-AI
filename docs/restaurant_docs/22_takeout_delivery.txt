MENU LIMITATIONS FOR TAKEOUT

Suitable for Takeout:
- Pasta dishes (best when reheated properly)
- Meat and seafood dishes (travel well)
- Appetizers (most items suitable)
- Desserts and gelato
- Wines and beverages

Not Recommended:
- Delicate soufflés or light mousses
- Some salads with dressing (dressing served separately)
- Carpaccio (raw items)
- Temperature-sensitive preparations

Special Instructions:
- Request sauce on side for pasta
- Dressing separate for salads
- Special heating instructions provided
- Chef accommodates most requests
