LA BELLA TAVOLA - DINING ETIQUETTE GUIDE

SILVERWARE & UTENSIL USAGE

Basic Rule:
- Work from outside inward with each course
- Utensils organized accordingly by course
- Different sizes for different courses
- No need to memorize - servers guide if uncertain

Fork Placement:
- Forks on left side of plate
- Larger fork (table fork) for main course
- Smaller fork (appetizer fork) on outside left
- Salad fork may be on far left

Knife & Spoon Placement:
- Knives and spoons on right side of plate
- Blades face inward (toward plate)
- Spoons outside of knives
- Largest knife for main course (outside)

Soup Spoon:
- Larger spoon on far right
- Move spoon away from you (toward 6 o'clock)
- Bring spoon to mouth from side
- Not lifted straight up from bowl

Bread Plate & Butter Knife:
- Bread plate on upper left
- Butter knife placed horizontally across bread plate
- Use butter knife to spread butter
- Not a full-size dinner knife for bread

Finished Course Indicator:
- Place fork and knife together diagonally on plate
- 4 o'clock position (toward lower right)
- Signals to server: "Please clear this course"
- Clean plate removed and next course brought

NAPKIN ETIQUETTE

Initial Placement:
- Napkin placed on lap shortly after seating
- Unfold napkin fully on lap
- Keep napkin on lap throughout meal
- Tuck corner into lap if needed (for protection)

During Meal:
- Use napkin to wipe mouth discreetly
- Never blow on food to cool
- Use napkin to dab corners of mouth
- Napkin stays on lap between courses

When Leaving Table:
- Napkin left on chair (not placed on table)
- Looser fold shows meal is continuing
- Signals server that guest is returning
- If meal finished, napkin placed to right of plate

At Meal End:
- Napkin loosely placed to right of plate
- Don't fold napkin back up formally
- Leave for server to clear
- Shows your appreciation for their work

DRINKING & BEVERAGE ETIQUETTE

Water Glass:
- Upper right of plate
- Sip before and after courses
- Refilled by server continuously

Wine Glass:
- Right of water glass (slightly larger)
- Hold by stem (not bowl)
- Prevents warming wine with hand heat
- Maintains temperature and flavor

Toasting Etiquette:
- Hold glass by stem
- Make eye contact with person
- Clink glasses gently
- Say "Cheers" or "Salud"
- Sip after toast

Finishing Glass:
- Drink entire glass or large amount
- Host may terminate rounds
- Never pressure others to drink
- Pace yourself with meal

EATING PACE & MANNERS

Pacing:
- Eat at conversational pace
- Don't rush through meal
- Don't linger excessively between courses
- Match pace of dining companions

Chewing:
- Chew with mouth closed always
- No audible chewing sounds
- Bite-sized pieces aid discretion
- Conversation pauses for chewing

Speaking While Eating:
- Never speak with mouth full
- Finish and swallow before responding
- Pause, swallow, speak sequence
- Shows respect for conversation

Portion Sizes:
- Cut pasta into manageable pieces
- Pasta eating can be neat with twirl technique
- Don't stuff mouth with large pieces
- Show restraint in portion bites

BREAD & SIDES ETIQUETTE

Bread Service:
- Bread offered at beginning of meal
- Eat while waiting for first course
- Break bread into smaller pieces
- Don't bite directly from loaf

Butter Application:
- Take small amount on personal bread plate
- Use bread plate butter knife
- Apply small amount to broken bread piece
- Spread with small knife

Pasta Eating:
- Twirl pasta on fork against spoon
- Or cut into manageable pieces
- Avoid loud slurping sounds
- Fork is primary utensil

Using Spoon with Fork:
- Hold fork in right hand
- Optional spoon in left hand (supporting)
- Twirl pasta onto fork
- Some find it easier, not required

CONVERSATION & SOCIAL ETIQUETTE

Table Conversation:
- Moderate voice level (don't shout)
- Avoid controversial topics
- Engage with dining companions
- Include everyone in conversation

Phone Usage:
- Phone on silent (not vibrate)
- Minimal phone checking
- Never answer during courses
- Step away if must take call

Compliments & Feedback:
- Compliment food to server warmly
- Mention specific favorite items
- Feedback about service appreciated
- Constructive criticism respects kitchen

Handling Mishaps:
- Spill or mistake: Handle gracefully
- Wave off server apologies
- Broken dish: Not a big deal
- Laugh it off, continue enjoying meal

DIETARY RESTRICTIONS & SPECIAL REQUESTS

Informing Server:
- Mention allergies or restrictions immediately
- Not impolite to inform kitchen
- Kitchen wants to keep you safe
- Clear communication appreciated

Asking Questions:
- Ask about ingredients without hesitation
- Ask about preparation methods
- Query about gluten, dairy, nuts, etc.
- Server or chef knowledgeable to answer

Modification Requests:
- Make requests politely
- Understand some modifications not possible
- Accept gracefully if not feasible
- Thank server for attempting accommodation

HANDLING FOOD ISSUES

If Food Arrives Wrong:
- Inform server immediately (not rudely)
- Describe what was ordered vs. delivered
- Server corrects without argument
- No additional charge for correction

If Food Tastes Off:
- Take a polite taste first
- Describe the issue (not just "I don't like it")
- Server or chef consults
- Replacement prepared if legitimate issue
- Consider if it's personal preference vs. problem

If Food Arrives Cold:
- Alert server promptly
- Politely request reheating
- Kitchen replaces without cost
- Doesn't reflect poorly on you

PAYMENT & CHECK HANDLING

Check Presentation:
- Check brought when requested (or naturally)
- Usually placed in folder on table
- Not rushed presentation
- You choose when to pay

Payment Method:
- Inform server of payment choice
- Card taken to be processed
- Or cash counted at table
- Card returned on tray with receipt

Leaving Gratuity:
- Tip 18-20% standard
- Written on receipt or left in cash
- Thank server briefly if departing
- Positive parting impression

LEAVING THE TABLE

Brief Absence:
- Excuse yourself politely
- Napkin left on chair (not table)
- Minimizes disruption
- Return promptly

Extended Absence:
- Inform server of brief departure
- Napkin on chair signals return
- Kitchen can hold preparation if needed
- Return within reasonable time

TAKING PHOTOS OF FOOD

Photography Etiquette:
- Food photos acceptable and common
- Brief moment for photos understood
- Don't create obvious delay
- No flash photography (respect mood)
- Single photo usually sufficient
- Then eat while hot/fresh

DRESS CODE REMINDERS

Appropriate Attire:
- Smart casual required
- Neat and clean appearance
- Restaurant may comment on violations
- Respectful of establishment

GREETING & DEPARTURE COURTESY

Upon Arrival:
- Smile and greet hosts warmly
- Eye contact with hosts
- Relaxed, friendly demeanor
- Positive energy appreciated

Thanking Staff:
- Thank server by name if known
- Specific compliments appreciated
- Friendly goodbye to hosts
- Positive parting makes their day

TIMING & LINGERING

Reasonable Dining Duration:
- Lunch: 1.5-2 hours typical
- Dinner: 2-3 hours typical
- Not rushed to leave
- But tables not held indefinitely

If Tables Needed:
- Server may tactfully mention
- Understand if prompted to leave
- Not personal, just restaurant needs
- Don't take offense

CHILDREN DINING ETIQUETTE

Setting Example:
- Teach children restaurant manners
- Model polite behavior
- Children follow parent example
- Minimal disruption expected

Managing Young Children:
- Keep children seated and quiet
- Crayons and activities help
- Praise good behavior
- Quietly address misbehavior

GENERAL COURTESY REMINDERS

Respect the Environment:
- Don't litter or mess floor excessively
- Respect other diners' experience
- Keep noise level reasonable
- Leave restaurant as found it

Respect the Staff:
- Servers are professionals, not servants
- Treat with kindness and respect
- Patient understanding appreciated
- Remember they're human too

Enjoy the Experience:
- Relax and enjoy meal
- Don't stress about perfection
- Restaurant staff understanding
- Genuine enjoyment shows in interactions
