MEDICAL EMERGENCY PROCEDURES

In Case of Medical Emergency:

Immediate Response:
1. Call 911 if serious medical event
2. Notify staff/manager immediately
3. Staff assist with AED if needed
4. First aid applied by trained staff
5. Comfortable position arranged

Staff Training:
- First aid certification maintained
- CPR training (some staff)
- AED (Automated External Defibrillator) on-site
- Emergency responder protocols practiced
- Regular training updates scheduled

Allergic Reaction Protocol:
- Immediate notification to kitchen/manager
- EpiPen location identified (kitchen/host stand)
- 911 called immediately for severe reactions
- Staff assistance if guest authorized
- Emergency responders following instructions
- Never minimize allergic reaction severity

Choking Protocol:
- Staff trained in Heimlich maneuver
- Immediate response if choking suspected
- 911 called if unable to dislodge
- Comfort and reassurance provided
- Follow-up medical evaluation afterward

FOOD-RELATED INCIDENTS

Allergic Reaction (Food):
- See previous allergic reaction protocol
- Inform kitchen immediately of allergen
- Never downplay severity
- Medical assistance prioritized
- Documentation for reference

Food Poisoning Suspicion:
- Contact physician or poison control
- Contact restaurant manager
- Describe symptoms and items eaten
- Timeline of symptoms documented
- Medical assessment recommended
- Restaurant follows up

Contaminated Food Discovery:
- Immediately inform server
- Replace dish without question
- No charge for replacement
- Inspect returned dish (manager)
- Investigation if necessary
- Prevent other guests' exposure

CHOKING PREVENTION

Common Choking Foods:
- Large pasta pieces (unlikely at restaurant)
- Bones in seafood (inform of possibility)
- Difficult textures
- Prevention responsibility: Guest
- Alert server if concern exists

If Choking Occurs:
- Alert staff immediately
- Heimlich maneuver applied by trained staff
- 911 called
- Continue attempts until breathing restored
- Emergency responders summoned
- Follow-up medical care

INJURY AT RESTAURANT

Slip & Fall:
- Inform staff immediately
- Assist to safe, comfortable location
- Ice or first aid if minor injury
- Manager documenting incident
- Medical assistance if needed
- Report incident details

Burn or Cut Injury:
- Cool water immediate application (minor)
- First aid kit available
- Medical assessment (manager)
- 911 if serious
- Incident documented
- Restaurant liability insurance notified

Other Injuries:
- Staff assistance provided
- Comfortable care prioritized
- Medical evaluation if needed
- Incident documentation
- Insurance notification if serious
- Follow-up communication

FIRE & EVACUATION

Fire Alarm Protocol:
- All guests evacuate immediately
- Follow staff leadership direction
- Exit via nearest safe exit
- Main entrance typical exit
- Gather in meeting area (specify)
- Staff account for guests
- Emergency responders arrival

Fire in Kitchen:
- Guests generally unaware
- Staff immediate response
- Suppression systems activated
- Evacuation IF needed (assessed by staff)
- Professional fire response
- Guests notified by manager

Fire Prevention:
- Kitchen equipment regularly maintained
- Fire suppression systems updated
- Staff trained in fire safety
- Exit routes clearly marked
- Emergency equipment accessible
- No fire hazards in dining area

NATURAL DISASTERS

Earthquake (if applicable):
- Drop, cover, hold on protocol
- Stay seated if tremor mild
- Move away from windows
- Secure breakable items mentally
- Remain calm
- Staff guidance followed

Severe Weather:
- Staff assess weather severity
- Continue service if safe
- Guests may shelter at restaurant
- Extended weather events: Call ahead

SECURITY INCIDENTS

Violent Situation:
- Call 911 immediately
- Follow staff instructions
- Comply with any demands (safety first)
- Memorize aggressor details if safe
- Cooperate with police investigation
- Support offered to affected guests

Theft:
- Inform staff immediately
- Description provided to manager
- Police report filed if significant
- Security footage reviewed
- Restitution pursued by restaurant
- Prevention measures enhanced

CUSTOMER BEHAVIOR INCIDENTS

Intoxicated Guest:
- Service can be refused
- Staff assist in arranging safe departure
- Alternate transportation offered
- Call taxi/ride-share if needed
- Alcohol service stopped
- Manager handles discretion

Disruptive Behavior:
- Staff address behavior professionally
- Polite request to stop behavior
- Manager consultation if continues
- Removal from restaurant possible
- Police called if threatening
- Trespass warning issued if necessary
