LA BELLA TAVOLA - GROUP BOOKING POLICY

GROUP RESERVATION DEFINITION

Group Size:
- Parties of 8 or more guests qualify as group reservations
- May be seated at single table or multiple tables
- One point of contact required for coordination
- Group booking deposit expected (see payment section)

BOOKING PROCESS FOR GROUPS

Initial Inquiry:
- Call restaurant: +1-800-555-1234
- Email: group@labellatatavola.com
- Manager or event coordinator will assist
- Details discussed: date, time, party size, menu preferences

Booking Procedures:
1. Provide guest count and approximate party size range
2. Confirm preferred dining date and time
3. Discuss seating arrangement preferences
4. Review menu options and special requests
5. Confirm deposit and payment terms
6. Receive confirmation with group details

Timeline Recommendations:
- 2+ weeks advance notice: Optimal planning
- 1-2 weeks: Accommodated if possible
- Less than 1 week: May have limited menu options
- Last-minute groups: Depends on availability

PARTY SIZE & PRICING

Standard Group Size: 8-30 guests
- Separate tables or large table arrangement
- Standard menu available
- Customized menu possible with notice
- Pricing per person basis or fixed menu price

Large Groups: 30+ guests
- Requires private event consideration
- Contact manager for availability
- May need to close restaurant section
- Customized menu strongly recommended
- Fixed menu pricing typical for groups this size

SEATING ARRANGEMENTS

Booth Seating:
- Large booth accommodates up to 8 guests
- Preferred for groups wanting unity
- Limited number of booths available
- Reserve early for booth seating

Table Combinations:
- Multiple tables arranged for group cohesion
- Flexible arrangement based on party composition
- Chef's counter available for smaller groups (up to 4)
- Window seating available if enough tables

Dining Room Sections:
- Back section can accommodate larger groups
- Slightly more private than main dining room
- Minimizes disruption to other guests
- Seating arrangement flexible within section

MENU OPTIONS FOR GROUPS

Standard Menu Ordering:
- Each guest orders individual selections
- Server takes orders and brings individual plates
- Suitable for groups wanting variety

Family-Style Service:
- Large platters shared among group
- Multiple pasta and meat dishes served
- Group decides on sharing approach
- Portion sizes adjusted accordingly

Prix Fixe Group Menu:
- Fixed menu selected for all guests
- Typically 3-4 courses
- Customizable within price point
- Minimum number of guests may apply

Customized Group Menu:
- Chef works with group coordinator
- Tailored menu for special themes or preferences
- Requires advance notice (1-2 weeks minimum)
- Special pricing available for customized menus

SPECIAL REQUESTS FOR GROUPS

Dietary Accommodations:
- Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free options available
- Request accommodations when booking
- Chef can prepare special dishes in advance
- Notify server of any changes closer to event

Allergies:
- Kitchen informed in advance for all group allergies
- Special prep procedures implemented
- Chef consulted for severe cases

Dietary Census:
- Request list of dietary needs before event
- Allows for proper advance planning
- Email to manager: manager@labellatatavola.com
- Update list if changes occur closer to event

GROUP PRICING & DEPOSITS

Deposit Requirement: $15 per person
- Non-refundable if cancelled less than 48 hours before
- Applied toward final bill if reservation honored
- Credit card required to hold deposit
- All major credit cards accepted

Deposit Timing:
- Due within 7 days of booking confirmation
- Online payment available
- Payment plan available for very large groups
- Confirmation email sent with payment details

Final Headcount:
- Confirm final guest count 48 hours prior
- Billing based on confirmed headcount
- Additional guests: Additional charges apply
- Fewer guests: Deposit adjustment discussed

PAYMENT FOR LARGE GROUPS

Billing Options:
1. Single check (group pays together)
2. Multiple checks (organized by host)
3. Per-person invoicing (each guest charged separately)
4. Partial pre-payment plus balance at event

Service Gratuity:
- Automatic 18% gratuity applied to groups 8+
- Calculated on pre-tax total
- Additional tip can be added by group
- Discussed with event coordinator at confirmation

Tax and Pricing:
- Food and beverages taxed per standard rate
- Gratuity separate from tax
- All pricing confirmed in writing before event
- Itemized invoice provided after event

CANCELLATION POLICY FOR GROUPS

Group Cancellations:
- Less than 48 hours: Deposit forfeited; full per-person cancellation fee applies
- 48+ hours: Deposit refunded, no cancellation fees
- Free cancellation for documented emergencies
- Manager considers special circumstances

Partial Cancellations:
- Reducing party size less than 24 hours: Reduced guest penalty applies
- Cancellation fees apply to removed guests
- Final headcount due 48 hours prior

Rescheduling:
- Changing date or time 24+ hours prior: No fees
- Date change less than 24 hours: May incur fees

SPECIAL OCCASIONS FOR GROUPS

Birthday Celebrations:
- Complimentary birthday dessert offered
- Custom cake can be brought in or arranged with chef
- Special lighting or announcement available
- Notification helpful when booking

Anniversary Groups:
- Anniversary recognition available
- Complimentary champagne toast for couple
- Special seating considerations
- Please notify when booking

Corporate Events:
- Team building dinners accommodated
- Customized menu options available
- Event planning coordination with manager
- Meeting space availability limited

TIMING & SERVICE

Group Dining Duration:
- Typical duration: 2.5-3.5 hours
- May vary based on course count
- Table held for reasonable dining period
- Extended dining requires advance discussion

Service Pace:
- Paced service for group dining
- Courses timed for synchronized service
- Courses brought together when possible
- Server coordinates timing with kitchen

Bar Service for Groups:
- Pre-dinner cocktail hour optional
- Bar tabs organized for group
- Bartender coordinates drink orders
- May need separate bar setup for very large groups

CONTACT & COORDINATION

Group Event Coordinator:
- Manager assigned to large events
- Single point of contact for group
- Responsive to questions and changes
- Present during event for oversight

Pre-Event Meeting:
- Recommended for groups 20+
- Walk-through dining room layout
- Review menu and service flow
- Confirm all special details

Communication:
- Email: group@labellatatavola.com
- Phone: +1-800-555-1234
- Group extension available for regular groups
- 24/7 emergency contact for large private events
