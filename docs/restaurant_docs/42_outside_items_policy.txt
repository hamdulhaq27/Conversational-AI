LA BELLA TAVOLA - OUTSIDE ITEMS & SPECIAL POLICIES

OUTSIDE CAKE POLICY

Bringing Outside Cake to Celebration:
- Welcome to bring outside cake
- At no additional cost
- Advance notice appreciated (48 hours)
- Plating and serving by restaurant staff
- Plates, forks, napkins provided

Cake Service Logistics:
- Cake stored in restaurant refrigerator
- Service at dessert course timing
- Staff plates and presents cake
- Candles available (ask server)
- Celebration song optional

Specialty Bakery Coordination:
- Restaurant-recommended bakeries available
- Alternative: Source yourself
- Restaurant can warm/adjust if needed
- Timing coordination important
- Delivery timing discussed

Cost & Service:
- No charge for outside cake service
- Tip for server appreciated (their labor)
- Appreciate their accommodation
- Small token gesture welcome
- Gratitude remembered

Restrictions:
- Some special events limit outside cake
- Check with manager if complex event
- Refrigerator space limited (advance notice helps)
- Health codes respected (sanitation)
- Reasonable restriction understood

OUTSIDE BEVERAGES POLICY

Alcohol:
- No outside alcoholic beverages permitted

Non-Alcoholic:

- Outside juices/sodas not permitted
- Special dietary beverages: Discuss with manager
- Medical condition beverages: Management considers
- Medications in beverages: Manager discretion

OUTSIDE FLOWER & DECORATION POLICY

Fresh Flowers:
- Personal arrangements welcome at table
- Reasonable size appreciated
- Vases provided by restaurant
- No damage to flower petals
- Cleanup responsibility understood

Balloons:
- Balloons welcome (reasonable quantity)
- Ribbon secure to prevent floating
- Not blocking other guests' view
- Removal after dining expected
- Helium balloons generally OK

Decorations:
- Simple decorations welcome

- Photography-focused: Plan accordingly
