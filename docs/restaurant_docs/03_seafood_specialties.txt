LA BELLA TAVOLA - SEAFOOD SPECIALTIES

SEAFOOD SOURCING & SUSTAINABILITY:
La Bella Tavola is committed to sustainable seafood practices. We work with responsible suppliers and rotate our specials based on seasonal availability and sustainability practices. Ask your server about our current sustainable choices.
