DURING SERVICE (5:30 PM - 11:30 PM)

Order Flow:
1. Server takes order
2. Order ticket enters kitchen
3. Tickets called out by expeditor
4. Multiple stations begin simultaneously
5. Timing coordinated

Coordination:
- Expeditor (sous chef or senior) calls orders
- Station accountability clear
- Communication via kitchen language
- Timing precise
- Multiple dishes coordinated
- "Ready" signals coordinated

Quality Control:
- Each plate inspected before passing
- Temperature verification
- Presentation standards met
- Flavor approved (sampling occasional)
- Only approved plates served

Plating & Presentation:
- Plating station organized
- Garnish application final
- Plate temperature proper
- Artistic presentation
- Speed without rushing
- Quality paramount

INGREDIENT STORAGE

Dry Storage:
- Pantry items organized by type
- Canned goods labeled and rotated
- Pasta varieties accessible
- Rice and grains
- Spices and seasonings
- Temperature and humidity controlled

Refrigerated Storage:
- Walk-in refrigerator organized by item type
- Raw seafood separate
- Proteins arranged clearly
- Vegetables sectioned
- Sauces properly stored
- Labeling and dating
- Temperature: 38°F maintained

Frozen Storage:
- Walk-in freezer (-18°C)
- Stock properly organized
- Proteins separated
- Rotation system (FIFO: First In, First Out)
- Labeling and dating critical
- Inventory tracking
