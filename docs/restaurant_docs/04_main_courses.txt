LA BELLA TAVOLA - MAIN COURSES

Our main course selection features premium ingredients prepared with classical Italian and Mediterranean techniques. Each plate is carefully plated and presented.

MEAT DISHES

Osso Buco alla Milanese
Braised veal shanks slowly cooked with white wine, vegetables, and broth until the meat falls off the bone. Served with gremolata and saffron risotto. A Milanese classic. Calories: 650 | Gluten-Free: Yes

Saltimbocca di Vitello
Thin veal cutlets layered with prosciutto and fresh sage, pan-seared and finished with white wine and herbs. Served with seasonal vegetables. Calories: 480 | Gluten-Free: Yes

Osso Buco is paired with Saffron Risotto as the traditional accompaniment.

Pappardelle al Cinghiale (see pasta section for full description)
Can also be served as a main course in larger portion.

Costolette di Agnello alla Griglia
Lamb chops grilled to perfection with rosemary, garlic, and lemon. Served with roasted potatoes and seasonal greens. Calories: 560 | Gluten-Free: Yes

Pollo alla Parmigiana
Breaded chicken breast layered with fresh mozzarella, tomato sauce, and Parmesan, baked until golden. Served with pasta or salad. Calories: 520 | Contains: Gluten | Vegetarian alternative: Available

Branzata di Manzo
Slow-braised beef brisket in Barolo wine with carrots, celery, and onions. Served with creamy polenta and braised greens. Richly flavored comfort food. Calories: 620 | Gluten-Free: Yes

Suckling Pig (Seasonal - Porceddu)
Whole suckling pig roasted until golden and crispy. Served with seasonal vegetables and bread. For 2+ persons. Pre-order recommended. Serves 4-6. Calories per serving: 580

POULTRY

Chicken Piccata
Tender chicken breast with capers, fresh lemon, and white wine sauce. Light and bright flavors. Served with your choice of sides. Calories: 420 | Gluten-Free: Yes

Duck Confit alla Toscana
Honey and herb-glazed duck leg confit served with cannellini beans and bitter greens. Rich and satisfying. Calories: 540 | Gluten-Free: Yes

MIXED GRILL & LARGE PORTIONS

Grigliata Mista
A selection of grilled meats including veal chop, lamb chop, sausage, chicken breast, and seafood. Served with roasted vegetables and your choice of two sides. For 2+ persons. Serves 2-4. Calories: 680 per person

Bollito Misto
Traditional celebration dish of mixed boiled meats served with seasonal vegetables, broth, and salsa verde. A festive and hearty preparation. For 2-4 persons. Pre-order recommended. Calories: 550 per person
