LA BELLA TAVOLA - PASTA DISHES

Our pasta selection features traditional Italian recipes paired with Mediterranean flavors. All pasta is made fresh in-house daily.

CLASSIC PASTA DISHES

Tagliatelle al Ragù Bolognese
Wide ribbons of fresh egg pasta with our signature meat ragù simmered for 4 hours with San Marzano tomatoes and aromatics. A traditional Bolognese preparation that honors the recipe from Emilia-Romagna. Calories: 580 | Contains: Egg, Gluten

Spaghetti ai Frutti di Mare
Fresh spaghetti tossed with mixed seafood including clams, mussels, shrimp, and squid in a light white wine and tomato sauce. Finished with fresh parsley. Calories: 420 | Contains: Shellfish, Gluten

Fettuccine Alfredo
Ribbon pasta coated in a rich cream sauce made with butter, Pecorino Romano, and fresh cracked black pepper. This simple classic requires the finest ingredients and expert technique. Calories: 520 | Vegetarian: Yes | Contains: Dairy, Gluten

Pappardelle al Cinghiale
Wide pappardelle ribbons with wild boar ragù, rosemary, and Chianti wine reduction. A Tuscan specialty highlighting game meat traditions. Calories: 550 | Gluten: Standard (Gluten-free available)

Linguine alle Vongole
Linguine with Manila clams in a garlic, white wine, and parsley sauce. Minimal ingredients allow the seafood to shine. A Roman classic. Calories: 380 | Contains: Shellfish, Gluten

Rigatoni alla Matriciana
Robust pasta tubes with guanciale (cured pork jowl), tomatoes, Pecorino Romano, and a touch of chili. Bold and satisfying. Calories: 480 | Gluten: Standard

Lasagna della Nonna
Traditional lasagna with béchamel sauce, Bolognese ragù, and ricotta cheese layered with fresh pasta sheets. Baked until golden and bubbly. Calories: 620 | Vegetarian option available | Contains: Dairy, Egg, Gluten

LIGHTER OPTIONS

Spaghetti al Pomodoro
Simple perfection: San Marzano tomatoes, fresh basil, garlic, and extra virgin olive oil. Let the tomatoes take the spotlight. Calories: 320 | Vegetarian: Yes | Vegan option: Yes | Gluten: Standard

Orzo "Risotto" with Mushrooms and Truffle
Creamy rice-shaped pasta prepared risotto-style with porcini mushrooms, black truffle oil, and Parmesan. Earthy and indulgent. Calories: 420 | Vegetarian: Yes | Gluten: Standard

Penne all'Arrabbiata
Quill pasta with San Marzano tomatoes, garlic, and red chili pepper. A quick Roman dish that packs bold flavor. Calories: 340 | Vegetarian: Yes | Vegan option: Yes | Gluten: Standard

SEAFOOD SPECIALTIES

Lobster Ravioli
Large handmade ravioli filled with sweet lobster meat, ricotta, and herbs, finished with a light saffron cream sauce. Garnished with lobster roe. Calories: 520 | Contains: Shellfish, Dairy, Egg, Gluten

Swordfish Pasta
Fresh swordfish steaks with capers, olives, and cherry tomatoes in a light lemon sauce. Served over fettuccine. Calories: 450 | Contains: Fish, Gluten
