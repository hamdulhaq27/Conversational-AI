LA BELLA TAVOLA - MENU MODIFICATIONS & CUSTOMIZATION GUIDE

CUSTOMIZATION PHILOSOPHY

Core Principle:
- Guest satisfaction through flexibility
- Reasonable modifications accommodated
- Kitchen capability respected
- Quality standards maintained
- Special requests within reason

When Modifications Work Best:
- Dietary restrictions and allergies
- Personal preferences (within bounds)
- Protein and sauce substitutions
- Cooking temperature adjustments
- Simple ingredient removals

When Modifications Are Challenging:
- Fundamental dish restructuring
- Complex recipe overhauls
- Multiple simultaneous modifications
- Advance preparation requirements
- Final-touch timing issues

FREQUENTLY ASKED CUSTOMIZATION QUESTIONS

Can you make this gluten-free?
Most dishes yes; confirmation needed. Pasta dishes yes (+$3). Some others may not be possible. Ask server.

Can you modify this significantly?
Yes if reasonable. Generally yes for dietary/allergy. Creative combination possible with kitchen consultation.

How much advance notice needed?
Simple requests same-day. Major customization 24+ hours preferred. Complex dishes 48+ hours. Best to call ahead for complex requests.

Is there an upcharge for modifications?
Usually no for simple changes. Gluten-free pasta +$3. Specialty ingredients may incur cost. Ask server.
