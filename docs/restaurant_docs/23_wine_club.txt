MONTHLY WINE SELECTIONS

Curation Process:
- Sommelier selects wines monthly
- Regional focus rotates quarterly
- Diverse styles and price points
- Supporting emerging and established producers
- Sustainability considerations included

Wine Selections Typically Include:
- 2-3 Italian wines (restaurant specialty region)
- 1-2 European wines
- Occasional non-European selections
- Mix of white, red, and sometimes rosé or sparklings
- Range from $25-$75 per bottle

Member Notification:
- Monthly wine announcement via email
- Tasting notes provided for each wine
- Pairing recommendations with menu items
- Food pairing suggestions
- Region and producer information

Ordering Process:
- Email notification of monthly selection
- Members have 10 days to order
- Minimum: 1 bottle per month
- Maximum: Varies by membership level
- Pickup or delivery to restaurant

Pricing:
- Standard member: 15% discount applied
- Premium member: 15% discount plus allocation benefits
- Collector: 15% discount, first pick of allocations
- Pricing lower than standard wine list pricing

WINE CLUB FAQ

How often do wines arrive?
Monthly selections; you choose what to order.

Can I gift a membership?
Yes, gift memberships available for 3, 6, or 12 months.

What if I don't like a wine?
Exchange for another selection available (within 14 days).

Can I store wines at restaurant?
Limited storage available for Collector members (discuss).

How do I access wines by delivery?
Third-party shipping coordinated; member to confirm address.

Are there any long-term commitments?
No, month-to-month with cancellation anytime.

Can I bring guest to events?
Yes, guests welcome (additional fee for non-members).
