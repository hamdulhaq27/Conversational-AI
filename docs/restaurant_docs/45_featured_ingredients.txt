Weekly Schedule:

- Weekend: Guest favorite or seasonal

- Brightness to richness
- Vegetable to meat emphasis
- Oil to butter shift

Autumn → Winter:
- Root vegetables
- Preserved items
- Game meats
- Slow cooking
- Warming spices

Winter → Spring:
- Stored items
- Artisanal products
- Preserved preparations
- Fresh greens return
- Brightness restoration
