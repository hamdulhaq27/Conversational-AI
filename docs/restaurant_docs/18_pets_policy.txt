LA BELLA TAVOLA - PETS POLICY

PETS AT LA BELLA TAVOLA

General Pet Policy:
- Service animals (guide dogs, service dogs) always welcome
- Certified emotional support animals welcome
- Typical house pets NOT permitted inside restaurant
- Exception: Outdoor seating (when available, weather permitting)

SERVICE ANIMALS

Definition:
- Dogs (and occasionally miniature horses) trained to perform specific tasks
- Tasks include guiding, alerting, mobility assistance
- Task-related disabilities required for classification
- Legal right to accompany handler in public spaces

Documentation:
- Service animal status verified by handler statement
- No special certification/vest/documentation legally required in most states
- Handler may be asked: "Is this a service animal?" and "What task does it perform?"
- Health/behavior of animal may be assessed

Dining Area Access:
- Service animals welcome in main dining room
- Seating accommodated for handler with animal
- Animal must remain under control and close to handler
- Allergies of other guests considered when seating

Behavior Expectations:
- Service animal must behave appropriately around food service
- Professional behavior and training expected
- Any aggressive behavior may result in removal
- Animal must not disrupt other guests' dining

EMOTIONAL SUPPORT ANIMALS

Distinction from Service Animals:
- ESAs provide comfort through companionship
- Do not perform specific trained tasks required
- Legal status varies by location/rental property
- May not have same public access rights as service animals

Restaurant Consideration:
- ESAs evaluated case-by-case
- Documentation (letter from mental health professional) may be requested
- Clean bill of health/vaccination expected
- Well-behaved demeanor required

Approval Requirement:
- Call in advance: +1-800-555-1234
- Discuss ESA and specific situation
- Manager approval required before arrival
- Documentation may be reviewed

Seating Arrangement:
- Quiet table preferred for ESA comfort
- Private dining area possible for sensitive cases
- Accommodation made if approved in advance
- Fee may apply for accommodation (discuss in advance)

OUTDOOR SEATING & PETS

Patio (When Available in Season):
- Dog-friendly seating area
- Clean bill of health/current vaccination expected
- Leashed or contained at all times
- Behavior monitored by staff

Rules for Outdoor Pets:
- Dogs only (no exotic pets)
- Well-socialized, non-aggressive
- Clean, well-groomed appearance
- Vaccination records may be requested

Water & Accommodations:
- Water bowl provided for dogs
- Shaded seating when available
- Staff monitors animal comfort
- Relief breaks accommodated

RESTRICTIONS & NOT PERMITTED

Not Permitted Inside:
- Cats (service cats with documentation very rarely approved)
- Rabbits, guinea pigs, other small animals
- Exotic birds and parrots
- Reptiles of any kind
- Ferrets or similar animals

Reasons for Restrictions:
- Health department regulations (most important)
- Other guest allergies and comfort
- Food safety and sanitation
- Potential behavior issues with wild animals

Violation Consequences:
- Animal asked to leave immediately
- No refund for meal if policy violated
- Possible ban from restaurant
- Health department could be contacted

DOCUMENTATION & HEALTH

Health Certificate:
- Current vaccinations required for all non-service animals
- Rabies vaccination particularly important
- Recent vet letter appreciated
- May be requested if manager has concerns

Cleanliness:
- Animals must be clean and well-groomed
- No obvious illness or parasites
- Good hygiene expected from owner
- Pet accident cleanup responsibility of owner

ADVANCE NOTICE

Recommended:
- Call restaurant at least 24 hours ahead: +1-800-555-1234
- Mention service animal, ESA, or pet accommodation request
- Allows staff to prepare and plan seating
- Provides opportunity to discuss any concerns

Benefits of Advance Notice:
- Quiet table can be reserved
- Staff prepared for animal
- Parking near entrance arranged if needed
- Special accommodations discussed

Day-of-Arrival:
- Mention animal/service animal at host stand
- Inform server immediately
- Any behavioral changes reported to management
- Proactive communication helps ensure smooth experience

ALLERGIES & CONSIDERATIONS

Other Guests:
- Restaurant attempts to balance pet and non-pet guest needs
- Separation of seating areas when possible
- Allergy-friendly seating for other diners
- Cross-contamination risks minimized

Allergy Notification:
- If allergic to animals, notify when making reservation
- Management works to accommodate both parties
- Most feasible solution preferred
- Sometimes compromise necessary

SPECIAL EVENTS WITH PETS

Wedding/Formal Events:
- Service animals tolerated in private dining
- Typical pets not typically accommodated for formal events
- Discuss specific circumstances with manager
- Custom arrangements possible

Casual Group Dinners:
- Service animals welcome at all group events
- Pets with advance approval possible for outdoor seating
- Manager approval required
- Behavior and cleanliness standards strict

RESTAURANT RESPONSIBILITY

Liability Consideration:
- Restaurant not responsible for pet behavior or incidents
- Owner fully liable for any damages caused by pet
- Guest assumes all risk bringing pet to restaurant
- Injury to pet: Guest assumes all responsibility

Staff Training:
- Staff trained on proper service animal interaction
- Trained NOT to pet or distract service animals
- Understand ESA vs. service animal distinction
- Professional interaction with animals expected

CLEANUP & ACCIDENTS

Accident Protocol:
- Owner responsible for cleanliness
- Staff assist with quick cleanup
- Surface sanitization performed immediately
- Extra care taken for health reasons

Damage Claims:
- Restaurant may charge for significant damage
- Cleaning costs charged to guest account
- Carpet replacement costs assessed if necessary
- Try to resolve amicably with management

QUESTIONS & CLARIFICATIONS

Before Arrival:
- Call restaurant with pet-related questions
- Manager happy to discuss specific situation
- Honest assessment provided
- Alternative restaurants recommended if can't accommodate

Common Questions:
- "Can I bring my dog?" - Only with advance approval for outdoor seating
- "Can I bring my certified service animal?" - Yes, always welcome
- "Can I bring my ESA?" - Maybe; call in advance to discuss
- "What if my pet has anxiety?" - Consider home care or pet sitter
