STORY BEHIND THE NAME

"La Bella Tavola" Translation:
- "The Beautiful Table" in Italian
- Evokes gathering around communal table
- Italian tradition of family meals
- Conveying warmth and welcome

Inspiration:
- Italian family dining traditions
- Long tables shared with family and friends
- Food bringing people together
- Creating memorable moments around food
