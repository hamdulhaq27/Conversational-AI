- Deposit and payment terms outlined

DEPOSITS & PAYMENT

Initial Deposit:
- 25% of estimated total due upon booking
- Holds date and catering team availability
- Non-refundable if cancelled less than 30 days before

Second Payment:
- 50% of estimated total due 30 days before event
- Confirms final guest count and menu

Final Balance:
- Balance due at conclusion of event
- Covers any final additions or adjustments
- Payment via cash or credit card
- Detailed invoice provided after event

CANCELLATION POLICY

Cancellation Terms:
- More than 30 days: Deposit refunded minus 10% processing fee
- 15-30 days: 50% of deposit forfeited
- Less than 15 days: Full deposit forfeited
- Day of event: Full payment due regardless

Rescheduling:
- Available depending on date availability
- Rescheduling treated as modification, not cancellation
- Deposit transferred to new date
