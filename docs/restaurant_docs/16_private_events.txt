- Menu customization options reviewed

PRIVATE EVENT PRICING

Space Rental:
- Dedicated private room: $500-$1,000 depending on size and time
- Back section: $300-$500 rental fee
- Full restaurant buyout: $2,500-$5,000 (pricing varies by timing)
- Rental fee applied toward food/beverage charges

Food & Beverage Minimums:
- Private room events: Typically $3,000-$8,000 minimum
- Varies based on guest count and menu selection
- Premium menu options increase minimum
- Alcohol consumption may add separate charges

Per-Person Pricing:
- Fixed menu: $45-$75 per person (food only)
- Premium menu: $75-$125 per person
- Wine pairings: Additional $40-$65 per person
- Beverages, tax, and gratuity added separately

Scheduling & Season:
- Weekday events: Standard pricing
- Weekend events: Premium pricing (Friday-Saturday add 15-20%)
- Holiday season: Peak pricing applies
- Flexibility available for off-season bookings

DEPOSITS & PAYMENT TERMS

Initial Deposit: 25% of estimated total
- Due within 14 days of confirmation
- Holds date and space reservation
- Applied toward final invoice

Second Payment: 50% of estimated total
- Due 30 days before event
- Confirms menu selections and final headcount
- Non-refundable if cancelled less than 30 days out

Final Balance: Remaining 25%
- Due at conclusion of event
- Covers any consumption above estimated charges
- Payment options: Cash, check, or credit card

Payment Plans:
- Available for large events (discussion with manager)
- Spread payments over 2-3 months
- All terms documented in writing

TIMING & SERVICE FLOW

Event Duration:
- Typical private event: 3-4 hours

Service Schedule:
- Cocktail hour: 30-60 minutes

CANCELLATION FOR PRIVATE EVENTS

Full Cancellation:
- More than 60 days: Deposit forfeited, no other charges
- 30-60 days: 50% of estimated total charged
- Less than 30 days: 100% of estimated total charged
- Documented emergencies: Case-by-case consideration

Postponement:
- May be available depending on date availability
- Rescheduling to different date: Deposit applied
- Same terms as cancellation apply for postponement
