MENU STRUCTURE

Standard Tasting Menu:
- 4 courses: Appetizer, Pasta, Main, Dessert
- Approximately 2.5-3 hours duration
- Wine pairing optional (see wine section)
- Portions: Balanced and satisfying
- Progression: Light to rich to sweet
- Pricing: $65 per person (food only)

Premium Tasting Menu:
- 5-6 courses: Extended experience
- Additional courses: Cheese course, palate cleanser
- Approximately 3-4 hours
- Wine pairing with each course
- Sommelier guidance throughout
- Pricing: $95 per person with wine pairings

COURSE PROGRESSION

Appetizer Course (First):
- Lightest course
- Palate awakening focus
- 1-2 items presented
- Flavors: Fresh and bright
- Purpose: Setting tone, excitement building
- Typical items: Carpaccio, delicate preparation

Pasta Course (Second):
- Central Italian course
- Handmade pasta likely
- Seasonal sauce focus
- Moderate richness
- Satisfying without heavy
- Typical items: Fresh pasta, seasonal sauce

Intermezzo (Optional):
- Palate cleanser between courses
- Light, refreshing element
- Sorbet common choice
- Temporary pause between richness
- Digestive aid function

Main Course (Third):
- Protein and vegetables
- Most substantial course
- Chef's specialty often featured
- Rich sauces acceptable
- Typical items: Fish, meat, vegetable preparations
- Seasonal protein showcased

Cheese Course (Premium Menu):
- Between main and dessert
- Regional Italian cheeses
- Flavors explored
- Bread or crackers
- Educational component
- Digestive benefits

Dessert Course (Final):
- Sweet conclusion
- Often two components
- Flavors: Balanced and refined
- Portion: Satisfying but not excessive
- Typical items: Chocolate, fruit, traditional Italian

Coffee & Petit Fours:
- After dessert service
- Espresso or coffee
- Small sweet bite
- Aperitivo option
- Graceful conclusion
