LA BELLA TAVOLA - RESERVATION POLICY

MAKING A RESERVATION

Phone Reservations: +1-800-555-1234
Hours: Monday-Sunday, 11:00 AM - 9:30 PM
Online Reservation System: Visit our website for instant reservation confirmation
Walk-in Policy: Walk-ins welcome during lunch hours; reservations strongly recommended for dinner, especially Fri-Sun

RESERVATION GUIDELINES

Reservation Confirmation:
- Online users receive instant confirmation
- Phone reservations confirmed immediately with confirmation number
- Confirmation details sent via email upon request
- We hold reservations for 15 minutes past reservation time

Party Size:
- Minimum party size: 1 guest
- Maximum party capacity per table: 8 guests
- Larger groups (9+ guests): Please call restaurant directly for special seating arrangements
- Groups of 10+ may be subject to prix fixe menu pricing

Advance Booking:
- Reservations accepted up to 60 days in advance
- Large group inquiries (15+ guests): Book 2+ weeks in advance for optimal seating
- Special occasions: Let us know about birthdays, anniversaries, or celebrations

CANCELLATION POLICY

Cancellation Procedure:
- Cancellations must be made at least 24 hours before reservation time
- Call +1-800-555-1234 to cancel
- Free canceling: No penalties for cancellations made 24+ hours in advance
- Late cancellations (less than 24 hours): Subject to a cancellation fee of $25 per person
- No-shows: Full party cancellation fee of $50 per person charged
- Exceptions made for emergencies upon case-by-case assessment

MODIFICATIONS

Reservation Changes:
- Date/time changes: Submit at least 24 hours in advance
- Party size adjustments: Available when made 24 hours prior
- Special requests: Add dietary restrictions, seating preferences, celebration notes

SPECIAL REQUEST OPTIONS

Dietary Requirements:
- Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, kosher - notify us at reservation or upon arrival
- Severe allergies: Please inform server immediately
- Pescatarian, paleo, low-carb - we can accommodate most dietary needs
- Last-minute accommodations made on best-effort basis

Seating Preferences:
- Window seating: Available on request (availability not guaranteed)
- Quiet tables: Suitable for romantic occasions
- Counter seating: Chef's counter reservations sometimes available for groups up to 4
- Accessibility needs: Please alert us so we can ensure proper accommodations

Celebration Requests:
- Birthday dining: Notify us at reservation for special recognition
- Anniversary celebrations: Let us know for complimentary dessert planning
- Engagements/proposals: Consult directly with management for customized experience
- Corporate events: See private events section

TIMING & DINING

Typical Dining Duration:
- Lunch service: 1.5 to 2 hours
- Dinner service: 2 to 3 hours (longer for tasting menus)
- Tables held for the duration of normal dining only
- Extended dining available upon availability and manager approval

Service Hours:
- Lunch: 12:00 PM to 3:30 PM daily
- Dinner: 5:30 PM to 11:00 PM daily (last seating at 10:00 PM)
- Kitchen closes at 11:30 PM
- Reservations made after operating hours will be contacted to confirm booking

OFF-SEASON & SPECIAL HOURS

- Holiday hours may vary; check website or call for special hour confirmations
- Seasonal closures limited to 2-3 days annually
- Private event bookings may close restaurant to public
- Summer hours occasionally extended

BOOKING ERRORS & DISPUTES

If Reservation Confusion Exists:
- Contact restaurant immediately with confirmation number
- We maintain detailed reservation logs
- Good faith effort made to accommodate all confirmed reservations
- In case of overbooked seating, manager offers complimentary drinks while waiting

ACCESSIBILITY & ADA ACCOMMODATIONS

- Restaurant fully accessible to guests with mobility limitations
- Wheelchair accessible entrance and restrooms
- Accessible seating available throughout dining room
- Service animals welcome; notify server upon arrival
- Large print menus available upon request

RESERVATION FREQUENCY & LOYALTY

- Returning guests acknowledged and valued
- Loyalty program available (see separate document)
- Regular reservations can establish preferred seating arrangements
- Special perks for frequent diners
