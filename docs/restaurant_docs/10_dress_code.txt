LA BELLA TAVOLA - DRESS CODE & DINING ETIQUETTE

DRESS CODE POLICY

Overall Dress Code: SMART CASUAL

What This Means:
Our dress code is designed to maintain an upscale, welcoming atmosphere while allowing guests to express their personal style. We ask that all guests dress neatly and appropriately for a nice restaurant setting.

SMART CASUAL STANDARDS

Recommended for All Guests:
- Collared shirts or blouses
- Dress pants, khakis, or nice jeans (without rips or holes)
- Closed-toe shoes
- Dresses or skirts of appropriate length
- Neat, well-groomed appearance

Acceptable Attire:
- Long-sleeve or short-sleeve collared shirts
- Polo shirts (in good condition)
- Sweaters or cardigans
- Blazers or sport coats
- Dress pants or chinos in neutral colors
- Closed-toe shoes (leather preferred)
- Nice flats, dress shoes, or loafers
- Scarves or modest jewelry appropriate to the outfit

NOT RECOMMENDED / NOT PERMITTED

Items We Prefer Guests Not Wear:
- Excessively casual athletic wear (gym clothes, yoga pants as primary garment)
- Graphic t-shirts with offensive or profane messages
- Beachwear or swimwear
- Overly ripped or distressed clothing (minimal wear acceptable)
- Flip-flops, slides, or beach sandals
- Baseball caps or casual hats (fedoras and formal hats acceptable)
- Extremely short shorts or skirts (mid-thigh or shorter not recommended)
- Tank tops or sleeveless shirts for men (sleeveless acceptable for women in appropriate attire)

EXCEPTIONS & SPECIAL OCCASIONS

Men:
- Tuxedos welcome for formal dinners or celebrations
- Sport coats recommended for special occasions
- Dress shoes preferred over casual sneakers
- Ties welcome but not required

Women:
- Evening gowns welcome for special occasions
- Cocktail dresses appropriate for upscale dining
- Dressy separates or pantsuits acceptable
- Heels, flats, or dress shoes all acceptable

SPECIAL EVENTS & PRIVATE DINING

Corporate Events:
- Business casual or business formal expected
- Specifics communicated with event coordinator
- Dress code may be more formal than standard

Wedding Celebrations & Special Occasions:
- Cocktail attire or formal wear recommended
- Specific dress codes communicated on invitations
- Guests asked to respect celebration formality level

CHILDREN & FAMILIES

Children's Dress Code:
- Smart casual applies to children as well
- Neat, clean clothing required
- School uniforms acceptable
- Comfortable but appropriate footwear recommended
- No significantly ripped or excessively casual athletic wear

Family Dining:
- Restaurant family-friendly during early hours
- Special accommodations made for families with young children

DINING ETIQUETTE GUIDELINES

General Conduct:
- Respect for other guests and staff is paramount
- Quiet, conversational volumes maintained
- Mobile phone use kept to minimum (must be silent)
- Photography of food acceptable; flash photography of other guests not appropriate

Table Manners:
- Silverware used from outside to inside with each course
- Napkins placed on lap
- Guests wait for all parties to be served before eating
- Chewing with mouth closed is respectful
- Compliments to chef appreciated; complaints addressed respectfully

With Staff:
- Servers treated with respect and courtesy
- Requests made politely
- Additional needs communicated clearly
- Staff helped when possible (e.g., pushing in chairs)

Bar & Cocktail Area:
- Respect for bartender's craft
- Reasonable cocktail modifications accommodated
- Tipping 18-20% standard for bar service
- Intoxication or disruptive behavior not tolerated

DRESS CODE ENFORCEMENT

Manager Discretion:
- Managers have discretion in dress code enforcement
- Egregiously casual or inappropriate attire may result in:
  - Polite request to modify presentation
  - Offer to reschedule reservation
  - In extreme cases, denial of service
- Management strives to be flexible while maintaining restaurant standards

If Turned Away:
- Rare and done respectfully by management
- Guests offered opportunity to return in appropriate attire
- No refund offered for policy violations
- Management happy to clarify expectations for future visit

SPECIAL CONSIDERATIONS

Religious or Cultural Attire:
- Religious head coverings welcome
- Cultural traditional dress respected and welcomed
- Communication with management about specific needs accommodated

Accessibility & Comfort:
- Mobility devices, wheelchairs, and canes welcome
- Adaptive clothing accommodated
- Staff trained to be respectful and discreet
- Comfortable, practical footwear for those with mobility concerns is fine

Professional Athletic Events:
- Guests coming directly from events accommodated when possible
- Professional team apparel generally acceptable
- Check with host if uncertain
