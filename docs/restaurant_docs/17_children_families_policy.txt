LA BELLA TAVOLA - CHILDREN & FAMILIES POLICY

CHILDREN AT LA BELLA TAVOLA

Welcome to Families:
- Children of all ages welcome
- Restaurant family-friendly for early dining
- Professional service accommodates families
- Positive dining environment for children

Age Guidelines:
- Infants (under 2): Welcome; high chairs available
- Toddlers (2-5): Welcome; children's menu available
- School-age (5-12): Welcome; youth menu offered
- Teenagers (12+): Welcome; full menu available

CHILDREN'S MENU

Simple & Familiar Options:
- Buttered pasta (plain or with tomato sauce)
- Grilled chicken breast with vegetables
- Meatballs with pasta
- Simple cheese pizza (available from kitchen)
- Grilled fish fillet with vegetables

Portion Sizes:
- Smaller portions appropriate for children
- Adjustable portion sizes available
- Half-portions of adult dishes often suitable
- Ask server for recommendations

Pricing:
- Children's menu: $8-$12 per entree
- Includes one side and choice of beverage
- Desserts available at regular pricing
- Beverages: Milk, juice, water, hot chocolate

HIGH CHAIRS & SEATING

High Chair Availability:
- Several high chairs available
- First-come, first-served
- Reserved upon advance request if possible
- Clean and well-maintained

Seating Preferences:
- Families with small children: Best seated away from main bar
- Quieter section: Helpful for young children if available
- Table positioning: Staff assist with comfortable arrangement
- Booster seats available for older toddlers

DINING PACE & ACCOMMODATIONS

Family Dining Pace:
- Service paced for family comfort
- Not rushed during extended children's dining
- Crayons and activity sheets available
- Patience expected with younger children

Early Meal Timing:
- Best to book early seatings (before 7:30 PM)
- Quieter, less crowded service
- Kitchen less backed up
- Children better behaved in less crowded environment

Bottle & Food Support:
- Warm water for formula bottles available
- Kitchen can warm baby food jars
- Nursing-friendly private areas available
- Changing table in restroom (check first)

SPECIAL SERVICES FOR FAMILIES

Birthday Celebrations:
- Children's birthdays welcomed
- Special acknowledgment by server
- Complimentary children's dessert on birthday
- Small party accommodations available

Allergy Information:
- Children's dietary needs carefully handled
- Special dishes prepared for allergies or restrictions
- Kitchen briefed in advance
- Separate prep area considerations

Photography:
- Family photos welcome
- Staff happy to take group photos
- Kids' meal plating can be made special for photos
- Birthday or occasion photos encouraged

BEHAVIOR EXPECTATIONS

Reasonable Conduct Expected:
- Normal children's behavior accommodated

- Running or excessive commotion managed
- Staff may kindly address disruptive situations

Language & Content:
- Restaurant assumes family-appropriate conduct
- Other guests present; noise kept reasonable
- Children taught restaurant respect
- Staff guide families in addressing issues

STAFF SUPPORT FOR FAMILIES

Friendly Service:
- Servers experienced with families

- Extra napkins and bibs provided
- Spills and accidents handled gracefully

Safety First:

- Hot beverages kept safe from children

Assistance Available:
- Ask for changing table locations

- Inquire about children's menu options

Portion Sizes:

Safe Choices:

LEAVING WITH CHILDREN

Payment with Children:

GENERAL TIPS FOR FAMILIES

Best Times to Visit:
- Lunch service: 12:00 PM - 3:00 PM (casual)
- Early dinner: 5:30 PM - 6:30 PM (quieter)
- Avoid peak dinner times: 7:00 PM - 8:30 PM

Dress Code for Kids:
- Smart casual standards apply (relaxed)
- School clothing acceptable
- Very casual acceptable for young children
- Neat, clean appearance expected

Making Reservations:
- Note "family with young children" when booking
- Request accommodating table/time
- Early seating beneficial
- Advance notice helps restaurant prepare
