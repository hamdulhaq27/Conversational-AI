LA BELLA TAVOLA - FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

RESERVATION QUESTIONS

How far in advance can I make a reservation?
You can reserve up to 60 days in advance through our reservation system.

What if I want to come as a walk-in?
Walk-ins welcome during lunch hours. For dinner, especially Fri-Sun, reservations recommended but walk-ins accommodated when space available.

Can I modify my reservation after booking?
Yes, call us at +1-800-555-1234 at least 24 hours before for changes to date, time, or party size.

What if I need to cancel?
Cancel at least 24 hours in advance for free cancellation. Less than 24 hours: cancellation fee of $25 per person. No-shows: $50 per person fee.

Do you hold the table for late arrivals?
Reservations held for 15 minutes past reservation time. Call if running late.

MENU QUESTIONS

Do you have a children's menu?
Yes, menu with smaller portions and kid-friendly options available.

Are there vegetarian options?
Yes, multiple vegetarian pastas, appetizers, and main course selections available.

Can I get gluten-free options?
Gluten-free pasta available (+$3). Many dishes naturally gluten-free. Notify server of severe celiac disease.

Is the restaurant nut-free?
No, nuts used in some preparations (almonds, pistachios, etc.). Inform server of nut allergies.

What about vegan options?
Yes, vegan pasta, risotto, and vegetable preparations available. Notify when ordering.

DINING EXPERIENCE

What time should I arrive for dinner?
Dinner begins 5:30 PM; last seating 10:00 PM. Peak times are 7-8:30 PM. Earlier seating less crowded.

What's the dress code?
Smart casual. Collared shirts, dress pants, or nice dresses. No athletic wear or beachwear. Neat appearance expected.

How long is a typical meal?
Lunch: 1.5-2 hours; Dinner: 2-3 hours. Not rushed, though tables not held indefinitely.

Can my group be split between tables?
Yes, if necessary. Request large single table or connected tables when making reservation.

Can we take photos?
Yes, food photos welcome. Flash photography of other guests not appropriate without permission.

ALCOHOL & BEVERAGES

Do you serve alcohol?
Yes, full bar with wine, beer, and cocktails available.

Can I bring my own wine?
No outside alcohol allowed. Full wine selection available from restaurant.

Do you have non-alcoholic cocktails?
Yes, mocktails available. Ask server for virgin versions of any cocktail.

How's the wine selection?
Extensive Italian wines with some international selections. Knowledgeable sommelier available for pairing recommendations.

Are happy hour specials available?
Yes, Mon-Fri 4-6 PM: appetizer specials and discounted drinks.

PAYMENT QUESTIONS

What payment methods do you accept?
Cash, all major credit cards, debit cards, and digital payment options (Apple Pay, Google Pay).

Do you add gratuity automatically?
Automatic 18% gratuity applies to parties of 8+. Otherwise, gratuity is guest's choice (suggested 18-20%).

Can we split the check?
Yes, multiple checks available. Let server know at time of ordering.

Do you offer discounts?
Loyalty program members get points and rewards. Happy hour pricing 4-6 PM. Lunch specials.

Are there any mandatory fees?
No mandatory fees. Tax and gratuity (optional) are separate from food prices.

SPECIAL SERVICES

Do you accommodate large groups?
Yes, groups 8+ accommodated. 10+ groups: call ahead for pricing and menu options.

Can we do a private event?
Yes, private dining rooms and sections available. Contact events coordinator: events@labellatatavola.com

Do you offer catering?
Yes, full catering available on-site and off-site. Email: catering@labellatatavola.com

Is a tasting menu available?
Yes, chef's tasting menu available (4 courses, $65; with wine pairings $95). 48+ hours advance notice required.

Can we have a surprise celebration?
Yes, notify us in advance. We can arrange special touches for birthdays, anniversaries, proposals, etc.

DIETARY & HEALTH

How are allergies handled?
Inform server immediately. Kitchen briefed on severity. Separate prep area used for severe allergies.

Are there dairy-free options?
Yes, some gelato is dairy-free, pasta with oil-based sauces, grilled fish with oil. Options limited.

Is there a kosher menu?
Modified items available with advance notice (48+ hours).

Can you accommodate low-carb diets?
Yes, grilled proteins with vegetable sides. Pasta-free options available.

ACCESSIBILITY

Is the restaurant accessible for wheelchairs?
Yes, fully accessible. Level entry, accessible seating throughout, and accessible restroom.

Are service animals welcome?
Yes, service animals always welcome. Emotional support animals: call in advance for approval.

Do you have a changing table?
Yes, in the restroom. Facilities family-friendly.

Street parking available. Valet parking Friday-Sunday evenings (complimentary with reservation). Paid lot in back.

Yes, multiple bus lines serve the downtown area. Accessible location via transit.
