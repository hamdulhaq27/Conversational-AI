LA BELLA TAVOLA - PARKING & TRANSPORTATION

PARKING OPTIONS

Street Parking on Main Street

Availability:
- Street parking directly in front of and around restaurant
- Ample spaces typically available except Friday-Sunday peak hours
- Two-hour limit during business hours (12 PM - 6 PM)
- Free parking after 6:00 PM and on Sundays (check local regulations)

Cost:
- Metered parking: $1.50/hour or $10/day maximum
- After-hours free (check posted signs)
- Validate parking ticket with restaurant if available

Duration:
- Parking ticket valid for duration of meal (typically 2-3 hours)
- Extended parking available if needed
- Overflow metered spots nearby on adjacent streets

Valet Parking Service

Availability:
- Friday, Saturday, Sunday evenings only
- Operating 5:30 PM - 10:30 PM
- Available for all guests, especially recommended for special occasions
- Large groups prioritized for valet service

Valet Pricing:
- Complimentary valet with restaurant reservation (Fri-Sun only)
- Drop-off and retrieval seamless
- Vehicles parked in secure lot one block away
- Tip appreciated ($2-$5 typical)

Reservation and Valet:
- Mention valet needs when making reservation
- Upon arrival, let host know you have valet parking reserved
- Valet staff will greet you at entrance
- No tipping required but appreciated

Accessible Parking (ADA)

Designated Spaces:
- One ADA-accessible parking space directly next to restaurant entrance
- Blue "wheelchair" symbol painted on pavement
- No other vehicles may park in accessible space
- Free parking, no time limit

Accessible Lot Parking:
- Small accessible space in rear lot as backup
- Reserved for guests with ADA placards/plates
- Direct elevator/ramp access to restaurant (see about elevator)

Accessible Parking Tips:
- Call ahead: +1-800-555-1234 to alert staff
- Arrive early if possible to ensure space availability
- Staff available to assist with mobility needs
- Parking lot attendant available Friday-Sunday for assistance

PARKING LOT (Behind Restaurant)

Location:
- Behind building, accessible from side street (Elm Street)
- Small lot with approximately 15 parking spaces
- First-come, first-served basis
- Free parking for all restaurant guests

Features:
- Well-lit for safety
- Security camera coverage
- Attendant on duty Friday-Sunday evenings
- Direct access to restaurant via side entrance

Limitations:
- Limited capacity; fills up during peak times
- Not guaranteed to have available spaces
- Overflow parking to street lot if full
- Valet recommended during busy Friday-Saturday

PUBLIC TRANSPORTATION

Bus Routes:
- Main Street bus stop nearby (Check local transit map for routes)
- Multiple bus lines serve downtown area
- Bus stop approximately 100 feet from restaurant
- Driver can point toward restaurant entrance

Transit Options:
- Downtown transit system: Routes 5, 12, 18, 21 serve area
- Check local transit authority for schedules
- Mobile apps available for real-time transit info
- Passes and fare information on transit website

Taxi & Ride-Share Services

Taxi Service:
- Multiple taxi companies serve downtown area
- Call dispatch or hail from street
- Restaurants staff can arrange taxi upon request
- Approximate cost to downtown location: $8-$12

Uber/Lyft/Ride-Share:
- Drop-off directly on Main Street
- Convenient for parties from surrounding areas
- Driver can be directed to restaurant entrance
- Recommended for evening service or after drinking

Directions for Driver:
- "123 Main Street, Downtown"
- Entrance on Main Street (no side street pickup)
- Valet attendant can guide Uber/Lyft vehicle

BICYCLING TO RESTAURANT

Bike Parking:
- Bike rack available at front entrance (6-8 spaces)
- Free secure bike parking
- First-come, first-served basis
- Staff can assist with bike security if needed

Bike-Friendly Access:
- Downtown bike lanes connect to restaurant
- Flat, easy access appropriate for most comfort bikes
- Well-lit area for evening cycling
- Helmets required by local law

PARKING FOR SPECIAL EVENTS

Large Group Parking:
- For parties of 30+, advance coordination recommended
- Contact manager to arrange valet expansion
- Multiple parking options coordinated
- Staff assigned to direct guests

Corporate Events:
- Event planner can coordinate special parking arrangements
- Valet service expanded for large groups
- Lot reservation possible for dedicated parking
- Attendant staff available for traffic flow

PARKING VALIDATION & DISCOUNTS

Parking Validation:
- Restaurant provides parking validation tickets
- Valid for discounted street parking
- Available for guests making meal purchase
- Ask server for validation details

Free Parking Incentives:
- Complimentary valet with reservation (Fri-Sun)
- Discounted valet rates for weekday events
- Loyalty program members: valet discount available
- Details available through reservation system

CARS LEFT OVERNIGHT

Overnight Parking:
- Not recommended for restaurant use
- Check local street parking regulations
- Night parking restrictions apply on Main Street
- Assess alternative parking if late dinner

PARKING ISSUES & COMPLAINTS

If Parking Problem Occurs:
- Contact restaurant: +1-800-555-1234
- Report valet issue to valet manager
- Tow situation: Contact police non-emergency for assistance
- Documentation needed for any reimbursement claims

Parking Lot Safety:
- Well-lit and secure area
- Security measures in place
- Staff assistance available
- Report concerns to manager immediately
