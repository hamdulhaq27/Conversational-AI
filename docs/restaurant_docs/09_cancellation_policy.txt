LA BELLA TAVOLA - CANCELLATION & NO-SHOW POLICY

CANCELLATION PROCEDURES

How to Cancel Your Reservation

Phone Cancellation: +1-800-555-1234
- Monday-Sunday: 11:00 AM - 9:30 PM
- Cancellation confirmed immediately
- Request confirmation number for records

Online Cancellation:
- Log into reservation system on our website
- Select reservation and choose "Cancel Reservation"
- Instant confirmation provided
- Email confirmation sent automatically

Email Cancellation:
- Email: reservations@labellatatavola.com
- Subject line: "Cancellation Request"
- Include reservation name, date, time, and party size
- Confirmation sent within 1 business hour

CANCELLATION TIMEFRAMES & FEES

24+ Hours Before Reservation:
- Free cancellation
- No fees charged
- Notice appreciated for inventory management

12-24 Hours Before Reservation:
- Cancellation fee: $15 per person
- Example: 4-person party = $60 cancellation fee
- Charge applied to original payment method
- Notice helps us manage seating

6-12 Hours Before Reservation:
- Cancellation fee: $25 per person
- Example: 4-person party = $100 cancellation fee
- Charge applied to original payment method
- Late notice significantly impacts our service

Less Than 6 Hours / No Notice:
- Cancellation fee: $50 per person
- Example: 4-person party = $200 cancellation fee
- Applies to full reserved party size
- No shows treated as cancellations with full fees

NO-SHOW POLICY

Definition of No-Show:
- Failure to arrive within 15 minutes of reservation time
- No call or cancellation made in advance
- Table held and meal service not able to be offered

No-Show Fees:
- $50 per person charged to credit card on file
- Example: 6-person reservation with no-show = $300 charge
- Charge processed within 48 hours
- Replaces lost revenue from unseated table

Multiple No-Shows:
- Two consecutive no-shows: A member of our team will contact you
- Three or more no-shows in a year: Account may be suspended
- Restaurant reserves right to request deposit for future reservations

EXCEPTIONS TO CANCELLATION FEES

Emergencies:
- Medical emergencies: Fees generally waived
- Family emergencies or deaths: Contact restaurant manager
- Severe weather preventing travel: Fees may be waived
- Documentation may be requested for waiver

Proof of Emergency:
- Hospital admission papers
- Police incident report
- Other official documentation
- Manager approval required

Contacting Management for Exceptions:
- Phone: +1-800-555-1234
- Request to speak with manager
- Explain circumstances
- Decision made on case-by-case basis
- Response provided within 24 hours

DEPOSIT REQUIREMENTS

When Required:
- Parties of 8+ guests
- Private events
- Holiday period reservations
- Large group bookings

Deposit Amount:
- $15 per person (non-refundable booking deposit)
- Or full meal cost estimate for private events
- Held in restaurant system pending reservation

Deposit Application:
- Applied toward final bill if reservation kept
- Forfeited if reservation cancelled less than 48 hours before
- Refunded if cancelled 48+ hours before reservation

Payment Methods:
- Credit card required to hold deposit
- All major credit cards accepted
- No cash deposits accepted

POLICY CLARIFICATIONS

Relationship to Party Size Changes:
- Increasing party size 24+ hours: No additional fee
- Decreasing party size 24+ hours: Cancellation fees apply to removed guests
- Example: 6-person cancels to 4 persons with less than 24 hours notice = $25 x 2 guests fee

Group Cancellations:
- Partial party cancellations treated as individual cancellations
- Full group cancellations subject to group policy (see group bookings)

Rescheduling vs. Cancellation:
- Moving date/time within 24 hours: May incur fees
- Rescheduling 24+ hours in advance: No fees
- Always confirm new date before cancelling old reservation

RECURRING RESERVATION CANCELLATIONS

Standing Reservations:
- Customers with weekly/monthly standing reservations
- If cancelling single occurrence: Notify 24+ hours before
- If cancelling standing reservation series: Contact manager
- Cancellation fees apply per normal policy unless standing arrangement specifies otherwise

CONTACTING CUSTOMERS ABOUT CANCELLATIONS

Restaurant-Initiated Cancellations:
- In rare circumstances, restaurant may need to cancel
- Full refund or alternative reservation date offered
- Customer contact made 48+ hours in advance when possible
- No fees charged for restaurant-initiated cancellations
