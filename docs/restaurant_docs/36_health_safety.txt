LA BELLA TAVOLA - HEALTH & SAFETY INFORMATION

FOOD SAFETY PROTOCOLS

Hygiene Standards:
- All staff required food safety certification
- Hand washing protocols strict (multiple times per shift)
- Clean and sanitized kitchen equipment
- Professional cleaning crews every evening
- Health inspector regular visits

Food Preparation:
- Temperature monitoring for hot foods
- Cold storage proper temperatures maintained
- Raw and cooked foods separated
- Cross-contamination prevention strict
- Proper food storage times observed

Allergen Handling:
- Allergen-sensitive items isolated
- Kitchen staff trained on cross-contamination risks
- Separate cutting boards for allergen items
- Special attention to shellfish and nut allergies
- Clear communication about allergen risks

PERSONAL HEALTH CONSIDERATIONS

When Not To Dine Out:
- If experiencing severe food allergies
- If immunocompromised (severe risk)
- If with contagious illness (respect other guests)
- If recently taken concerning medications

Allergy Communication:
- Inform server of allergies immediately
- Chef personally consulted for severe allergies
- Explain severity level (discomfort vs. anaphylaxis)
- Documentation of allergy may be kept

Dietary Restrictions:
- Religious dietary needs accommodated
- Medical dietary restrictions honored
- Ethical dietary preferences respected
- Special accommodations discussed in advance

ILLNESS & FOODBORNE ILLNESS CONCERNS

If Illness Suspected:
- Contact restaurant manager immediately
- Document specifics: When ate, what eaten, symptoms
- Inform restaurant of any illness after visit
- Provide phone number for follow-up

In Case of Emergency:
- Call 911 if severe allergic reaction
- Staff trained in emergency response
- AED available in restaurant
- First aid kit and trained staff present

Prevention Tips:
- Eat within safe temperature parameters
- Don't ignore obvious food concerns
- Trust your instincts on food quality
- Inform server immediately of problems

DIETARY INFORMATION & CALORIC CONTENT

Nutritional Information:
- Available upon request for some items
- Not all items have precise nutrition data
- Chef and management can discuss general caloric content
- Estimate provided for customizations

Caloric Information in Menu:
- Marked on web menu when available
- Ask server for nutritional info
- General ranges provided for dishes
- Customization affects nutritional value

Low-Calorie Options:
- Light preparations available
- Grilled options lower fat
- Oil-based instead of butter when requested
- Vegetable-focused dishes lighter
- Ask chef for light preparation suggestions

RELIGIOUS & CULTURAL DIETARY LAWS

Kosher Dining:
- Modified menu items possible
- 48+ hour advance notice required
- Manager and chef consulted
- Limited options but accommodations made
- Cost may reflect special preparation

Halal Considerations:
- No pork used in restaurant (though Italian cuisine includes pork)
- Seafood available as halal-friendly
- Alcohol-free beverages available
- Wine cooking (alcohol reduces during cooking)
- Discussion with chef regarding specific needs

Buddhist/Hindu Practices:
- Vegetarian options plentiful
- Beef and pork both served (but not required)
- Vegetarian menu available
- Accommodations straightforward

OTHER HEALTH-RELATED TOPICS

Gluten-Free Dining Safety:
- Gluten-free pasta available
- Cross-contamination possible in shared kitchen
- Severe celiac disease: Chef consultation recommended
- Separate prep area can be arranged with advance notice
- Risk acceptance necessary due to shared kitchen space

High-Sodium Content:
- Italian cuisine uses some salt
- Low-sodium preparation available (request)
- Cooking style emphasizes flavor over salt reduction
- Blood pressure concerns: Ask chef about preparations

Dairy & Lactose Issues:
- Dairy-free options limited
- Many dishes contain dairy or cream
- Restaurant can reduce or eliminate dairy
- Advance notice helps preparation
- Lactose-free milk not available (lactose-intolerant guests must clarify needs)

PREGNANCY-RELATED CONSIDERATIONS

Safe Food During Pregnancy:
- Raw foods: Caesar dressing may contain raw egg (notify kitchen)
- Soft cheeses: Burrata and ricotta generally safe, avoid unpasteurized
- Deli meats: Prosciutto is cured (generally safe)
- Alcohol: None recommended during pregnancy
- Caffeine: Limit coffee and caffeinated beverages

Foods to Avoid:
- Undercooked meats: All meats cooked well-done
- Raw seafood: Not served (carpaccio is raw but beef)
- Raw eggs: Tiramisu contains raw eggs (notify kitchen)
- Unpasteurized dairy: Ask about pasteurization
- High mercury fish: Limited fish selections available

Special Accommodations:
- Extra water and hydration beverages
- Rest accommodation (quiet table)
- Bathroom location knowledge helpful
- Extra bathroom breaks accommodated
- Flexibility with reservations for health needs

CHILD & INFANT HEALTH CONSIDERATIONS

Baby Food:
- Restaurant can warm purchased baby food
- Ask server to heat carefully to safe temperature
- Sterilized bottles preferred for safety
- Changing table available in restroom

Formula & Bottles:
- Hot water available for formula preparation
- Microwave availability for bottle warming
- Clean bottles essential (ask for clean cup if needed
- Boiled water available upon request

Allergen Awareness:
- Child allergies: Inform server immediately
- Separate children's preparation if needed
- Kitchen staff briefed on child's allergies
- Extra care with small children and allergens

SENIOR & ELDERLY GUEST CONSIDERATIONS

Dietary Issues:
- Softer preparations available
- Denture-accommodating meals (softer proteins)
- Portion size adjustments
- Slower paced service helpful

Medication Interactions:
- Certain foods may interact with medications
- Guest responsibility to know interactions
- No alcohol with certain medications
- Restaurant understands dietary supplements

DIGESTIVE HEALTH

Spice Sensitivity:
- Most dishes not spicy
- Kitchen can reduce spice
- Italian cuisine generally not heavily spiced
- Cream reduces spice perception

Fat Digestion:
- Rich creamy dishes may be difficult
- Oil-based sauces lighter alternative
- Grilled items easier to digest
- Server can suggest light preparations

Fiber Considerations:
- Vegetable-based sides (good fiber)
- Whole grain options limited
- Pasta lower in fiber
- Vegetable-heavy meals available

ALLERGY EMERGENCY PROTOCOLS

EpiPen Administration:
- Staff trained in emergency response
- Emergency responders called (911) immediately
- Staff can assist if directed
- Never delay professional medical care
- Honesty about allergy severity paramount

Anaphylaxis Response:
- 911 called immediately
- First aid provided by trained staff
- Guest directed to safest position
- EpiPen used if available and guest authorized
- Follow emergency responder instructions

Minor Allergic Reactions:
- Antihistamines available (guest may have)
- Water and calm environment provided
- Manager consulted
- Emergency services called if worsening
- Hospital trip assessed for necessity

POST-MEAL STOMACH ISSUES

Mild Discomfort:
- Often resolves within hours
- Overeating can cause discomfort
- Rich foods may cause temporary issues
- Over-the-counter remedies helpful

Serious Symptoms:
- Contact physician or poison control
- Describe symptoms and what was eaten
- Timeline of symptom onset helpful
- Professional medical help sought
- Contact restaurant for discussion if concerning

GENERAL WELLNESS RECOMMENDATIONS

Before Dining:
- Eat light meal before heavy restaurant meal
- Hydrate properly before arrival
- Don't arrive overly hungry
- Manage stress before dining

During Dining:
- Eat slowly and chew thoroughly
- Don't overeat (stop when satisfied)
- Moderate alcohol consumption
- Pace yourself with meal

After Dining:
- Rest after heavy meal
- Moderate physical activity
- Proper hydration continued
- Monitor for any issues
