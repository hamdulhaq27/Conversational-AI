LA BELLA TAVOLA - APPETIZERS MENU

Welcome to our appetizers selection. All appetizers are designed to awaken your palate and showcase the finest Italian-Mediterranean flavors.

COLD APPETIZERS (Antipasti Freddi)

Carpaccio di Manzo
Thinly sliced raw beef with olive oil, lemon, capers, and fresh parmesan. Presentation includes arugula and seasonal greens. Calories: 240 | Gluten-Free: Yes

Camarones al Limone
Chilled shrimp marinated in fresh lemon juice, olive oil, garlic, and Italian herbs. Served with citrus segments and crispy crackers. Calories: 185 | Contains: Shellfish

Burrata and Heirloom Tomatoes
Creamy burrata cheese from Puglia paired with fresh heirloom tomatoes, basil oil, and aged balsamic reduction. Gluten-Free: Yes | Vegetarian: Yes

Marinated Olives Selection
A curated selection of green and black olives from different Italian regions, marinated with herbs, citrus, and garlic. Gluten-Free: Yes | Vegetarian: Yes

Prosciutto di Parma
Premium imported prosciutto served with cantaloupe and fresh figs. Additionally available with grissini breadsticks. Calories: 200 | Gluten-Free: Yes (without breadsticks)

WARM APPETIZERS (Antipasti Caldi)

Calamari Fritti
Tender squid lightly breaded and fried until golden. Served with lemon wedges and homemade marinara sauce for dipping. Calories: 320 | Contains: Shellfish

Arancini Siciliani
Golden-fried risotto balls filled with ragù, mozzarella, and peas. Crispy exterior with creamy center. Vegetarian: Yes | Calories: 280

Bruschetta al Pomodoro
Toasted ciabatta bread topped with fresh tomatoes, garlic, basil, and extra virgin olive oil. Classic preparation highlighting Mediterranean flavors. Calories: 180 | Vegetarian: Yes

Mushroom Pappardelle Crostini
Wide ribbon pasta on toasted bread with sautéed mushrooms, truffle oil, and microgreens. Gluten-Free: Available on request | Vegetarian: Yes | Calories: 220

Melanzane alla Parmigiana
Roasted eggplant layered with fresh mozzarella, basil, and marinara sauce, baked until golden. Rich and comforting preparation. Vegetarian: Yes | Calories: 310

Scallops al Forno
Pan-seared diver scallops with garlic, white wine, and fresh herbs. Finished with brown butter. Calories: 240 | Contains: Shellfish | Gluten-Free: Yes

Pricing and Details:
Most appetizers range from $8-$14 and are designed as 2-3 person shareable portions. We recommend 2-3 appetizers per party for a complete experience. All items can be made gluten-free or vegetarian with advance notice.

Allergen Notice:
Contains: shellfish (camarones, calamari, scallops), gluten (most items unless specified), nuts (may vary by item).
Please inform your server of any dietary restrictions or allergies.
